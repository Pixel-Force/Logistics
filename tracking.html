Understanding Your Telematics Milestones

How Vanguard provides transparent custody tracking at every step of international transit.

Electronic Waybill (e-AWB / e-BL)

Issued the second your booking is confirmed. Enables customs authorities to pre-screen documentation prior to vessel or aircraft arrival.

Active Satellite Telematics

Direct data feeds from airline flight computers, vessel AIS transponders, and GPS trailer beacons calculate accurate dynamic ETAs.

Digital Proof of Delivery (e-POD)

Timestamped signature, consignee identification, and condition inspection photos uploaded instantly upon final unloading.
