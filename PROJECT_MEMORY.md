## Architecture & Technology Stack
- **Framework**: Semantic HTML5, Modular CSS3 with Custom Properties (CSS variables), Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Key Modules**:
  - `css/main.css` — Global styling, typography, colors, layout grid, CSS custom properties.
  - `css/components.css` — Corporate header, sticky navigation, dropdowns, interactive hero rate & tracking widgets, cards, timelines, forms, modal dialogs, toast notifications.
  - `css/responsive.css` — Mobile-first breakpoints, off-canvas navigation, touch ergonomics.
  - `js/main.js` — Navbar scroll state, mobile menu drawer, animated counters, FAQ accordions, toast notifications, newsletter handling.
  - `js/tracking.js` — Interactive live shipment tracking engine with mock database of realistic international waybills (`VEX-8924-US`, `VEX-1042-DE`, `VEX-5581-SG`, `VEX-3390-AE`), interactive milestone progression, checkpoint status, and bill of lading generation.
  - `js/calculator.js` — Instant Freight Rate Estimator with volumetric weight formulas, fuel surcharges, customs brokerage estimations, and instant booking handoff.
- **Complete Page Directory (12 Pages)**:
  1. `index.html` — Corporate Homepage with interactive Hero Rate/Track Widget, KPI strip, Services Grid, Why Choose Us, Interactive Hub Map, Industry Solutions, Workflow Steps, Testimonials, Case Studies, News/Insights, and CTA.
  2. `services.html` — Comprehensive Freight & Logistics Services Directory with interactive category filters, and Service Level Agreement (SLA) comparison table.
  3. `air-freight.html` — Dedicated Air Freight Service page with aircraft fleet payload specs (B747-8F, B777F, A330-300P2F), service tiers (NFO, Express, Economy), and instant booking.
  4. `ocean-freight.html` — Dedicated Ocean Freight Service page (FCL/LCL, Reefer, standard container specs 20ft, 40ft, 40ft HC, 40ft Reefer, major maritime corridors).
  5. `road-transport.html` — Road Freight & Intermodal rail haulage, telematics-tracked fleet, temperature-controlled trailers, and GLEC green rail savings.
  6. `warehousing.html` — Smart Contract Logistics & 3PL Warehousing, automated ASRS storage, pick & pack, distribution hubs, and WMS REST/EDI API integration.
  7. `tracking.html` — Full-featured Shipment Tracking Portal with interactive waybill search, live visual progress bar, transit checkpoints, and customs clearance status.
  8. `quote.html` — Instant Freight Rate Calculator & Comprehensive RFQ Booking Engine with itemized cost summary and booking modal.
  9. `network.html` — Global Port & Hub Directory (Americas, EMEA, APAC), interactive terminal selector, fleet specifications.
  10. `case-studies.html` — Real-world Supply Chain Case Studies with quantifiable efficiency gains and zero-compromise metrics.
  11. `about.html` — Corporate Overview, Mission & Vision, Executive Leadership, ESG & Green Logistics, Safety & Global Accreditations (ISO 9001:2015, IATA, FIATA, C-TPAT).
  12. `contact.html` — Global Offices (Chicago, Rotterdam, Singapore, Dubai), 24/7 Operations Control Center, Emergency Dispatch, Interactive Contact Form.
