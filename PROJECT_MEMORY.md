## Architecture & Technology Stack
- **Framework**: Semantic HTML5, Modular CSS3 with Custom Properties (CSS variables), Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Key Modules**:
  - `css/main.css` — Global styling, typography, colors, layout grid, CSS custom properties.
  - `css/components.css` — Corporate header, sticky navigation, mega-menu, interactive hero rate & tracking widgets, cards, timelines, forms, modal dialogs, toast notifications.
  - `css/responsive.css` — Mobile-first breakpoints, off-canvas navigation, touch ergonomics.
  - `js/main.js` — Navbar scroll state, mobile menu drawer, animated counters, FAQ accordions, toast notifications, newsletter handling.
  - `js/tracking.js` — Interactive live shipment tracking engine with mock database of realistic international waybills, interactive milestone progression, checkpoint status, and bill of lading generation.
  - `js/calculator.js` — Instant Freight Rate Estimator with volumetric weight formulas, fuel surcharges, customs brokerage estimations, and instant booking handoff.
  - `js/quote-wizard.js` — Multi-step Request For Quote (RFQ) generator with step-by-step validation and reference confirmation.
- **Pages**:
  - `index.html` — Corporate Homepage with interactive Hero Rate/Track Widget, KPI strip, Services Grid, Why Choose Us, Interactive Hub Map, Industry Solutions, Workflow Steps, Testimonials, Case Studies, News/Insights, and CTA.
  - `about.html` — Corporate Overview, Mission & Vision, Executive Leadership, ESG & Green Logistics, Safety & Global Accreditations.
  - `services.html` — Full Freight & Logistics Services Directory with interactive category filters.
  - `air-freight.html` — Dedicated Air Freight Service page with aircraft fleet specs, service tiers, and instant rate booking.
  - `ocean-freight.html` — Dedicated Ocean Freight Service page (FCL/LCL, Reefer, RoRo, vessel specifications, major port corridors).
  - `road-transport.html` — Road Freight & Intermodal rail haulage, telematics-tracked fleet, temperature-controlled trailers.
  - `warehousing.html` — Smart Contract Logistics & 3PL Warehousing, automated storage, pick & pack, distribution hubs.
  - `tracking.html` — Full-featured Shipment Tracking Portal with interactive waybill search, live visual progress bar, transit checkpoints, and customs clearance status.
  - `quote.html` — Instant Freight Rate Calculator & Comprehensive RFQ Booking Engine.
  - `network.html` — Global Port & Hub Directory (Americas, EMEA, APAC), interactive terminal selector, fleet specifications.
  - `case-studies.html` — Real-world Supply Chain Case Studies with quantifiable efficiency gains.
  - `contact.html` — Global Offices, 24/7 Operations Control Center, Emergency Dispatch, Interactive Contact Form.
