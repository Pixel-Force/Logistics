Architects of Modern Global Trade

Founded with a relentless dedication to precision and velocity, Vanguard Global Logistics provides the physical infrastructure and digital intelligence behind today's most resilient enterprises.

Our Heritage & Mission

Transforming Physical Supply Chains Through Modern Intelligence

Headquartered with major operations in Chicago, Rotterdam, Singapore, and Dubai, Vanguard manages over 1.4 million TEU and 450,000 metric tons of air cargo annually.

We operate under a simple ethos: supply chains should be completely transparent, fully digitized, and immune to single-point failures. Our interconnected telematics platform ensures our clients possess real-time control over their international commerce.
