Logistics, Rates & Freight Customs Intelligence

Everything you need to know about booking, tracking, customs duties, and cargo insurance with Vanguard.

In air freight and express courier services, freight charges are calculated based on whichever is greater: the actual scale Gross Weight or the Volumetric (Dimensional) Weight. Volumetric weight is calculated using the formula: (Length × Width × Height in cm) / 6000. This accounts for low-density, bulky cargo occupying significant aircraft cargo hold volume.

We maintain certified in-house licensed customs brokers in North America (C-TPAT / CBP), the European Union (AEO), the United Kingdom (HMRC CDS), and Asia. Shippers can elect Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) or Delivered at Place (DAP). We manage HS code classification, automated electronic EDI declaration filing, tariff concessions, and duty payment outlays.

Through our SatNet telematics system, customers receive real-time GPS coordinates, ETA variance calculations, port checkpoint milestones, customs clearance status, and for sensitive reefer or cold chain consignments: internal ambient temperature, humidity, and door-open shock sensor alerts.
